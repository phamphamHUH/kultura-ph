# Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao
*BARMM · Mindanao · Regional center: Cotabato City*

## Overview
BARMM is the heartland of Islam in the Philippines, with rich artistic traditions in dance, music, textiles and metalwork. Cultural groups include the Maguindanaon, Maranao, Tausug, Yakan, Sama and Iranun, each with its own distinct heritage. Sulu is no longer part of the region and is listed under Region IX in the PSGC as of 3Q 2026.
