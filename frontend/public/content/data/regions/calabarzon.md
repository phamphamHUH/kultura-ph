# CALABARZON
*Region IV-A · Luzon · Regional center: Calamba, Laguna*

## Overview
Close to Manila, this region was the heartland of Tagalog culture and of the 1896 Revolution. It is known for devotional festivals, fine woodcarving and a strong tradition of artisan towns.

## Provinces
- Cavite
- Laguna
- Batangas
- Rizal
- Quezon

## Languages and Peoples
- Tagalog (including Batangueño and Caviteño varieties)
- Bikol (parts of Quezon)

## Festivals
- **Pahiyas Festival** (Lucban, Quezon, May 15)
- **Higantes Festival** (Angono, Rizal, November)
- **Turumba Festival** (Pakil, Laguna)
- **Anilag Festival** (Laguna)
