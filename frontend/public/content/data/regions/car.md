# Cordillera Administrative Region
*The Cordilleras · Luzon · Regional center: Baguio City*

## Overview
Home to many Indigenous peoples, often grouped together as Igorot, the Cordilleras are known for centuries-old rice terraces, strong community rituals and resistance to colonization. Culture here is tied closely to land, ancestors and the agricultural calendar.
