# Caraga
*Region XIII · Mindanao · Regional center: Butuan City*

## Overview
Butuan is home to ancient balangay boats and the Golden Tara, evidence of an early trading kingdom. Today the region is better known for surfing in Siargao, though its Manobo and Surigaonon cultures remain central.
