# Central Visayas
*Region VII · Visayas · Regional center: Cebu City*

## Overview
This is where Christianity was first introduced in 1521 and where the Santo Niño devotion remains strong. Cebu has long been a commercial center, while Bohol is known for its blood compact history with Spain and its baroque stone churches. Since 2024, Negros Oriental and Siquijor belong to the Negros Island Region.
