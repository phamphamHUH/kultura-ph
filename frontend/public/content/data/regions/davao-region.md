# Davao Region

_Region XI · Mindanao · Regional center: Davao City_

## Overview

Davao's culture is shaped by 11 Indigenous ethnolinguistic groups collectively called the Lumad, alongside Visayan, Chinese and Japanese settler communities. Kadayawan, a thanksgiving festival, honors both harvest and this diversity.
