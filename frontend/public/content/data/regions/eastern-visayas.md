# Eastern Visayas
*Region VIII · Visayas · Regional center: Tacloban City*

## Overview
The region has a proud Waray identity, a distinct language, and a history of resilience to some of the strongest typhoons on record. Tacloban's festivals draw on body-painting traditions that recall pre-colonial Pintados.
