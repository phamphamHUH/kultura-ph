# MIMAROPA

_Region IV-B · Luzon · Regional center: Calapan, Oriental Mindoro_

## Overview

A region of islands. Mangyan communities in Mindoro preserve an indigenous script that is still in use, while Marinduque's Moriones is among the most recognized Lenten traditions in the country.

## Provinces

- Marinduque
- Occidental Mindoro
- Oriental Mindoro
- Palawan
- Romblon

## Languages and Peoples

- Tagalog
- Hanunuo Mangyan
- Cuyonon
- Palawano
- Romblomanon
- Tagbanua

## Festivals

- **Moriones Festival** (Boac, Mogpog and Gasan, Marinduque, Holy Week)
- **Baragatan sa Palawan** (Puerto Princesa, Palawan, June/July)

## Festivals with Tentative Dates

- **Biniray Festival** (Romblon, Romblon)
- **Pandurucan Festival** (Calapan, Oriental Mindoro)

## Food

- Tamilok (shipworm), a Palawan delicacy
- Palawan cashew
- Seafood of Coron and El Nido
- Marinduque arrowroot cookies (uraro)

## Crafts and Arts

- Mangyan ambahan poetry and Surat Mangyan script
- Romblon marble carving
- Palawan Tagbanua and Palawano woven crafts

## Heritage Sites

- Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park (UNESCO)
- Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park (UNESCO)
