# National Capital Region
*Metro Manila · Luzon · Regional center: Manila*

## Overview
The national capital is the country's political, economic and cultural hub. Its people come from every province, so Metro Manila is a meeting place of regional traditions, layered over Spanish colonial, American-era and Chinese-Filipino history (Binondo is among the oldest Chinatowns in the world).
