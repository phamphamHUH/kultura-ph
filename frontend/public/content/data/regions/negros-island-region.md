# Negros Island Region

_NIR (Region 18) · Visayas · Regional center: Bacolod City and Dumaguete City_

## Overview

Created by Republic Act 12000 in 2024, the Negros Island Region brings together Negros Occidental, Negros Oriental and Siquijor. Negros Occidental, once part of Western Visayas, is known for its sugar-era heritage and the MassKara Festival. Negros Oriental and Siquijor, once part of Central Visayas, are known for Dumaguete's university-town culture, Apo Island, and Siquijor's folklore. Hiligaynon and Cebuano are both widely spoken.

## Provinces

- Negros Occidental (including Bacolod City)
- Negros Oriental
- Siquijor

## Languages and Peoples

- Hiligaynon (Ilonggo)
- Cebuano
- Kinaray-a (southern Negros Occidental)

## Festivals

- **MassKara Festival** (Bacolod City, October)
- **Panaad sa Negros Festival** (Negros Occidental, April)
- **Buglasan Festival** (Negros Oriental, October)
- **Sandurot Festival** (Dumaguete City, September)
