# Northern Mindanao

_Region X · Mindanao · Regional center: Cagayan de Oro City_

## Overview

A meeting point of Indigenous groups and Visayan settlers. Bukidnon's Kaamulan gathers seven indigenous tribes in celebration, while Camiguin is known for its volcanoes and its community-run festivals.

## Provinces

- Bukidnon
- Camiguin
- Lanao del Norte
- Misamis Occidental
- Misamis Oriental

## Languages and Peoples

## Festivals

- **Kaamulan Festival** (Malaybalay, Bukidnon, February-March)
- **Lanzones Festival** (Camiguin, October)
- **Higalaay Festival** (Cagayan de Oro, August)
- **Diyandi Festival** (Iligan City, September)
