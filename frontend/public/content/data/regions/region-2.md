# Cagayan Valley

_Region II · Luzon · Regional center: Tuguegarao City_

## Overview

One of the most linguistically diverse parts of Luzon. Batanes, the northernmost province, has a distinctive Ivatan culture adapted to typhoons, while the valley shows Ibanag, Itawis and Ilocano influences.

## Cultural Stories

### Crafts and Arts

**Summary:**

Ivatan craftsmanship is shaped by the demanding weather and landscape of Batanes. Clothing and building practices use local materials and designs developed to help communities live safely and comfortably in a typhoon-prone island environment.

**Detailed Story:**

Vakul and kanayi are traditional Ivatan garments and protective headwear made from plant fibers, especially the leaves of the voyavoy palm. The vakul protects the head and shoulders, while the kanayi is a protective garment worn over the body. Their forms are practical responses to strong sun, wind and rain, and the knowledge of making them is part of Ivatan cultural identity. Ivatan stone-house building traditions use thick limestone walls and carefully constructed roofs to withstand harsh weather. These houses are not simply shelters: they reflect generations of knowledge about local materials, construction and the conditions of island life.

### Food

**Summary:**

Cagayan Valley's food traditions draw from river valleys, farms and island communities. Noodles, root crops, preserved ingredients and regional sausages reveal how local cooks make use of what is available in their surroundings.

**Detailed Story:**

Pancit batil patong, associated with Tuguegarao, is a hearty noodle dish usually served with meat, vegetables and an egg, accompanied by a savory broth. Its generous toppings make it a filling local specialty. Ivatan uvud balls are made using the pith or heart of banana or other locally available plant materials, commonly mixed with fish or other ingredients and shaped into balls before cooking. Ivatan luñis is a traditional pork preparation cooked and preserved in its own fat, while vunes refers to preserved gabi (taro) stalks, often prepared as a flavorful vegetable dish. Both demonstrate ways of storing food for later use. Longganisa of Cagayan Valley refers to local sausage varieties whose seasoning and preparation differ among communities, reflecting the region's mix of food influences.

### Heritage Places

**Summary:**

The region's heritage ranges from the stone-built villages of Batanes to caves, coastal islands and engineering landmarks. These places tell stories of adaptation, settlement, natural history and travel.

**Detailed Story:**

The Batanes stone houses and Sabtang are among the clearest expressions of Ivatan adaptation to a landscape exposed to strong winds and typhoons. Their thick stone walls and traditional roof forms preserve practical building knowledge as well as a distinctive village character. Callao Cave in Peñablanca is a large limestone cave system known for its chambers and dramatic rock formations; it is also associated with important archaeological discoveries in the area. Palaui Island is valued for its rugged coastline, beaches, marine environment and historic lighthouse landscape, making it a place where natural and maritime heritage meet. The Magapit Suspension Bridge crosses the Cagayan River and serves as a notable transport link, representing the infrastructure that connects communities across the valley.
