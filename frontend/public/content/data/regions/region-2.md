# Cagayan Valley
*Region II · Luzon · Regional center: Tuguegarao City*

## Overview
One of the most linguistically diverse parts of Luzon. Batanes, the northernmost province, has a distinctive Ivatan culture adapted to typhoons, while the valley shows Ibanag, Itawis and Ilocano influences.
