# Central Luzon
*Region III · Luzon · Regional center: San Fernando, Pampanga*

## Overview
Known as the Rice Granary of the Philippines and as the culinary capital of the country, thanks to Kapampangan cooking. Bulacan played a central role in the 1898 Malolos Congress, while Aeta communities in the highlands maintain some of the oldest cultures in the archipelago.
