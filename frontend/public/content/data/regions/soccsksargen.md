# SOCCSKSARGEN

_Region XII · Mindanao · Regional center: Koronadal City_

## Overview

A region of diverse communities. T'nalak cloth is woven by T'boli women following designs said to come from dreams, and is a recognized Intangible Cultural heritage. General Santos is the country's tuna capital.

## Cultural Stories

### Crafts and Arts

**Summary:**

SOCCSKSARGEN's craft traditions highlight the artistic knowledge of T'boli and B'laan communities. Textile weaving, metalwork and beadwork carry designs and techniques that express cultural identity and community knowledge.

**Detailed Story:**

T'nalak abaca weaving by T'boli dreamweavers is a highly regarded textile tradition. T'boli weavers prepare abaca fibers and create intricate patterns in a process that requires patience, skill and deep familiarity with the material. Designs are traditionally associated with images received in dreams, giving the cloth cultural and spiritual significance beyond its use as fabric. T'boli brass casting produces objects and ornaments by shaping molten brass in molds, requiring careful preparation and control of the casting process. B'laan beadwork uses small beads arranged into decorative patterns on clothing, accessories and personal ornaments. The colors and designs can communicate cultural identity and aesthetic preferences, while the work sustains specialized skills among makers.

### Food

**Summary:**

The region's food reflects its fertile agricultural areas and its connection to the waters around General Santos. Fresh seafood and tropical crops are central to the local table and economy.

**Detailed Story:**

Fresh tuna sashimi and kinilaw in General Santos showcase the quality and availability of tuna landed through the city's fishing industry. Sashimi is served raw in carefully prepared slices, while kinilaw uses an acidic marinade—often vinegar or citrus—along with seasonings to prepare fresh fish. Bananas are an important agricultural product across the region, enjoyed fresh and used in snacks, desserts and processed foods. Pili refers to the nut and fruit of the pili tree, which can be eaten roasted or incorporated into sweets and other food products. Together, these foods show the connection between local agriculture, fishing livelihoods and regional food culture.

### Heritage Places

**Summary:**

SOCCSKSARGEN's heritage landscapes include lakes, waterfalls, a volcanic mountain and an archaeological site. They preserve natural environments while offering glimpses into the lives and histories of communities in the region.

**Detailed Story:**

Lake Sebu in South Cotabato is a highland lake surrounded by communities, farms and forested landscapes. It is closely associated with T'boli culture and serves as an important setting for local livelihoods and tourism. The Seven Falls of Lake Sebu are a series of waterfalls in the area, celebrated for their scenic beauty and the surrounding landscape. Mount Matutum is a prominent volcanic mountain near General Santos and South Cotabato, valued for its forests, biodiversity and importance to the surrounding environment. The Maitum anthropomorphic burial jars in Sarangani are archaeological artifacts shaped with human-like features. They provide evidence of ancient burial practices and demonstrate the artistic and cultural complexity of communities that lived in the area long before the present day.
