# SOCCSKSARGEN
*Region XII · Mindanao · Regional center: Koronadal City*

## Overview
A region of diverse communities. T'nalak cloth is woven by T'boli women following designs said to come from dreams, and is a recognized Intangible Cultural heritage. General Santos is the country's tuna capital.
