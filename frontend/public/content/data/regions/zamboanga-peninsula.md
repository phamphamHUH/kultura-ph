# Zamboanga Peninsula
*Region IX · Mindanao · Regional center: Pagadian City*

## Overview
Zamboanga is the only place in Asia with a Spanish-based creole language, Chavacano, spoken widely in Zamboanga City. The peninsula is home to Subanen, Sama, Tausug and Visayan-descended communities. In the Philippine Standard Geographic Code (PSGC) as of 3Q 2026, Sulu is listed under this region.
